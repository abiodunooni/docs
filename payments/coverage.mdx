Retrieve currencies from the API rather than hard-coding them. New currencies and networks then reach your integration without a release.

```bash
curl 'https://api.basqet.com/v1/currency?type=CRYPTO' \
  -H 'Authorization: Bearer sk_test_YOUR_SECRET_KEY'
```

## Pricing currencies

You price a transaction in fiat, and the customer pays the equivalent in crypto.

| Currency | Code | Minimum |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nigerian naira | `NGN` | 2,000 |
| United States dollar | `USD` | 1 |

<Warning>
  The Initialize Transaction API accepts `NGN` and `USD`. Minimums are also published for `EUR` and `GBP`, but those are not currently listed as accepted pricing currencies — confirm with your Quidax representative before building against them.
</Warning>

## Payment currencies

What your customers can pay with.

| Currency | Code | Network |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tether | `USDT` | BEP-20 (BNB Smart Chain) |
| USD Coin | `USDC` | BEP-20 (BNB Smart Chain) |
| Bitcoin | `BTC` | Bitcoin |
| Litecoin | `LTC` | Litecoin |
| Ethereum | `ETH` | BEP-20 (BNB Smart Chain) |
| Quidax Token | `QDX` | BEP-20 (BNB Smart Chain) |

<Warning>
  **ETH is listed on BEP-20, not Ethereum mainnet.** Confirm what this means for your integration before you show it to customers — a customer who sends ETH from Ethereum mainnet to a BEP-20 address can lose their funds permanently. This entry is pending confirmation.
</Warning>

The same currencies and networks are supported for payouts. Sending to an address on a different network than the one shown loses the funds — there is no recovery.

## Minimum amounts

Minimums are enforced against the fiat currency the transaction is priced in, not the crypto the customer pays with.

| Currency | Minimum |
| --- | --- |
| `NGN` | 2,000 |
| `USD` | 1 |

They apply when you initialize a transaction and when you create a fixed-amount payment link. For links where the customer enters their own amount, the minimum is checked when the transaction is initialized, so validate the amount in your own interface first.

<Note>
  An amount below the minimum currently returns a `500` rather than a validation error. A clearer message is on the way. Until then, check the amount against the minimum before you call the API.
</Note>

Times depend on bank processing and blockchain network conditions.

## Settlement fees

Every settlement carries a fee that varies by currency. You choose who bears it:

| Option | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Merchant pays | The fee comes out of your settlement amount. |
| Customer pays | The fee is added to the amount collected at checkout. |

<Tip>
  Settling in a stablecoin carries a gas cost of roughly 1 USDT. If you quote customers exact dollar amounts, account for it — settling a 4 USD payment in USDT does not land 4 USDT in your wallet.
</Tip>
