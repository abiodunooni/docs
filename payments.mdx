Payments, powered by Basqet, lets you collect money from customers in crypto and settle it as crypto or local currency. Integrate once, and you get address generation, on-chain monitoring, conversion and settlement without running your own wallet or blockchain infrastructure.

## Choose a channel

Three ways to collect. They share one merchant account and one set of keys, so you can run more than one at a time.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Checkout SDK">
    A hosted payment modal you embed in your site or app. The customer picks a currency, sees an address and a QR code, and pays without leaving your checkout.

    **Best for** getting to market quickly with a standard checkout.

    **Effort** low. Drop in `basqet-checkout` and open the modal.

    <Card title="SDKs and checkout" icon="window" href="/payments/sdks" horizontal />
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Payment links">
    Shareable links that collect payment with no code at all. Create them in the dashboard or through the API, as a fixed amount or an amount the customer enters.

    **Best for** invoicing, social commerce and teams without a developer.

    **Effort** none for the dashboard, one API call if you want to generate them.

    <Card title="Payment links" icon="link" href="/payments/payment-links" horizontal />
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="API">
    Build the payment experience yourself. Initialize a transaction, initiate it against a chosen currency, then show your customer the address and amount.

    **Best for** custom checkouts, embedded wallets and bespoke reconciliation.

    **Effort** medium. Two REST calls plus a webhook handler.

    <Card title="Accepting payments" icon="code" href="/payments/pay-ins" horizontal />
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## How a payment works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Initialize">
    You create the transaction with the customer's email, an amount and a pricing currency. It comes back as `INITIATED` with a reference.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Initiate">
    The customer picks what to pay with. Basqet locks the rate, generates a wallet address and a QR code, and the transaction moves to `PENDING`.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Customer pays">
    They send the exact amount to the address. Basqet watches the chain and confirms it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="You get told">
    A webhook fires with the outcome — paid, underpaid, overpaid or abandoned. Give value on the webhook, not on the customer returning to your site.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Settle">
    Funds land in your Basqet balance. Settle them to a bank account or a wallet, automatically or on request.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Start building

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up and complete KYB. You can build in test mode straight away — KYB is only needed to switch to live.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get your keys">
    Copy your public and secret keys from the dashboard under Developers. Each environment has its own pair.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take one payment">
    Follow the quickstart to run a single transaction from initialize to settled.

    <Card title="Quickstart" icon="rocket" href="/payments/quickstart" horizontal />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Handle webhooks">
    Verify the signature, respond `200` immediately, and process events idempotently.

    <Card title="Webhooks" icon="webhook" href="/payments/webhooks" horizontal />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Test and launch">
    Simulate every outcome in test mode, then work through the go-live checklist.

    <Card title="Testing" icon="flask" href="/payments/testing" horizontal />
  </Step>
</Steps>
