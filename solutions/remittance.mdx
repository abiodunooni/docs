Collect from a sender in one market, deliver to a recipient in another, in minutes rather than days. Stablecoin carries the value between the two legs, so you never wait on correspondent banking.

## The architecture

```
Sender pays        →   value held as       →   Recipient receives
local currency         stablecoin              local currency
   (Ramp on-ramp)      (Wallets)               (Ramp off-ramp)
```

Three products, one account, one secret key.

| Leg | Product |
| --- | --- |
| Collect | [Ramp](/ramp) on-ramp, or a [Wallets](/wallets) address if the sender already holds crypto |
| Hold and move | [Wallets](/wallets) — internal transfers are instant and free |
| Deliver | [Ramp](/ramp) off-ramp to bank or mobile money |

## Why stablecoin in the middle

The value spends its time in flight denominated in dollars, not in either local currency. That removes the FX exposure that normally sits between collection and payout, and it means the payout leg does not depend on a correspondent bank being open.

<Note>
  Each corridor settles in its own local currency to a local account. Aggregating across currencies would place FX risk on Quidax on your behalf — hold stablecoin and convert on your own treasury side instead.
</Note>

## Build it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Collect from the sender">
    Ramp on-ramp, settled to a wallet you control.

    **Credit the transfer as funded** only on `buy_transaction.successful` with `data.status: completed` — not on `processing`.

    <Card title="Collect fiat, settle in stablecoins" icon="arrow-right-arrow-left" href="/use-cases/collect-fiat-settle-stablecoins" horizontal />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Move value to the payout side">
    Internal transfer between your own accounts — instant, free, no chain involved. Set `fund_uid` to the destination account ID.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Deliver to the recipient">
    Ramp off-ramp to a bank account or mobile money.

    **Expect** a name check when you attach the payout account. Accounts must belong to the transacting party.

    <Card title="Fund with stablecoins, pay out fiat" icon="building-columns" href="/use-cases/fund-stablecoins-payout-fiat" horizontal />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What will bite you

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Limits are per customer per rolling day" icon="gauge-high">
    Not per merchant, not per calendar day. A sender who transacts at 15:00 regains that headroom at 15:00 the next day, and there is no endpoint for remaining headroom.

    For remittance this matters more than most use cases — senders often want to send again the same week. Track consumption yourself.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Payout accounts must match the recipient" icon="id-card">
    Payouts go to accounts belonging to the person on the transaction. Collect recipient details accordingly from the start.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A payout can reverse after acceptance" icon="rotate-left">
    Uncommon but real. Watch for `sell_transaction.failed` and have a process for collecting an alternative account.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Mobile money versus bank rails" icon="mobile">
    In Ghana, mobile money reaches most consumer endpoints and settles in under five minutes; bank rails are slower but carry higher caps. Consumer remittance usually leads with mobile money.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Prohibited countries" icon="ban">
    Some jurisdictions are off-limits on every Quidax product, and US and Chinese persons are not onboarded regardless of where they transact.

    <Card title="Prohibited countries" icon="ban" href="/get-started/coverage#prohibited-countries" horizontal />
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
