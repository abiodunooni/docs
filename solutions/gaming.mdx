Gaming and betting products live or die on two moments: how quickly a player can deposit, and how quickly they get paid when they win. Both are payment problems.

## What you need

| Moment | Product |
| --- | --- |
| Player funds in local currency | [Ramp](/ramp) on-ramp |
| Player funds in crypto | [Wallets](/wallets) addresses, or [Payments](/payments) checkout |
| Player balance | [Wallets](/wallets) sub-accounts, or your own ledger |
| Winnings to a bank account | [Ramp](/ramp) off-ramp |
| Winnings to a wallet | [Wallets](/wallets) withdrawals |

## Build it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fund the player">
    Ramp on-ramp for local currency, or a Wallets address if they hold crypto.

    **Credit the player balance only on the settlement webhook** — `buy_transaction.successful` with `data.status: completed`, or a deposit at `ACCEPTED`.

    Crediting on `processing` or on `deposit.successful` alone means crediting money that can still be held or reversed. In gaming that becomes a player who has already staked funds you never received.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Run the balance">
    Either hold it in a Wallets sub-account, or hold your own ledger and keep funds pooled in your treasury account.

    Sub-accounts give you automatic deposit attribution. A pooled model means you reconcile deposits to players yourself, which is the part that goes wrong.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pay winnings">
    Off-ramp to a bank account, or a Wallets withdrawal to a wallet address.

    Generate a unique `reference` per payout before you call, and resolve anything uncertain by reference rather than resubmitting.

    <Card title="Payouts" icon="paper-plane" href="/payments/payouts" horizontal />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Handle the batch case">
    Tournament and jackpot payouts arrive all at once. Stay inside 300 requests per minute, submit one at a time, and make the batch resumable with deterministic references.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What will bite you

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Payouts cannot be reversed" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    An on-chain payout to a wrong address is gone. There is no address-validation endpoint, so validate format client-side and consider a small test send for unusually large wins.

    The 6-second cancellation window is not a customer-facing undo.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Double-crediting on webhook retries" icon="fingerprint">
    The same event can arrive more than once. In gaming a double credit is immediately exploitable — key every credit on the transaction ID and check before giving value.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Limits are per player, per rolling day" icon="gauge-high">
    Not per merchant, and not per calendar day. A player who deposits at 15:00 regains that headroom at 15:00 the next day.

    There is no endpoint for remaining headroom, so track it yourself if you want to warn a player before a deposit is rejected mid-session.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Underpayments on crypto deposits" icon="scale-unbalanced">
    Players routinely send slightly less than quoted because their wallet deducts a fee. Decide up front whether you credit the shortfall or refund it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Licensing" icon="scale-balanced">
    Gaming and betting are regulated separately from payments, and holding player funds is its own regulated activity. Confirm both positions during onboarding.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Speed expectations

| Leg | Typical |
| --- | --- |
| On-ramp funding | Under 5 minutes |
| NGN payout | Under 2 minutes |
| GHS mobile money payout | Under 5 minutes |
| Internal transfer | Instant |

Nigeria's instant rail runs continuously. Some bank rails have processing hours and queue outside them.
