Price in the currency your customers think in. Let them pay in whatever they hold. Receive one currency, with no chargebacks and no acquirer in the middle.

## What you need

| Step | Product |
| --- | --- |
| Checkout | [Payments](/payments) — SDK, links or API |
| Settlement | [Payments](/payments) settlement settings |
| Paying sellers | [Wallets](/wallets), or the dashboard with a CSV |

## Pick a channel

| | Checkout SDK | Payment links | API |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Effort | Embed a modal | None | Two calls plus webhooks |
| Single-use payments | Yes | **No — links are reusable** | Yes |
| Best for | Storefronts | Social selling, invoices | Custom checkouts |

For a storefront, use the checkout modal. It handles the currency picker, address, QR code and countdown — the parts customers get wrong — and you receive the same webhooks either way.

<Warning>
  Payment links are reusable. A static link can be paid many times, which is wrong for a single order. If you need one payment per order, initialize a transaction directly.
</Warning>

## Build it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Initialize when the customer reaches checkout">
    `POST /v1/transaction` with the amount, fiat currency and email. **Public** key.

    Put your order ID in `meta` — it comes back on every webhook.

    Validate the amount against the minimum first (₦2,000 or $1). Below it you currently get a `500`, not a validation error.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Initiate when they choose a currency">
    `POST /v1/transaction/{id}/pay`. **Secret** key.

    **The rate locks here and a 60-minute window starts.** Call it when they are ready to pay, not on page load — a customer browsing for an hour will find the transaction expired.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Reserve stock, do not sell it">
    The transaction is `PENDING` until the money arrives. Hold the stock, and release it on `payment.abandoned`.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fulfil on payment.received">
    Never on the customer returning to your site. Someone who closes the tab after paying still paid.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Settle into one currency">
    Turn on conversion so everything arrives in the currency you actually want to hold.

    <Card title="Settlements" icon="building-columns" href="/payments/settlements" horizontal />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What is different from cards

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="No chargebacks" icon="shield-check">
    Payments are final once confirmed on-chain. That removes a category of fraud loss, but disputes are resolved by you rather than an acquirer — write a refund policy before launch.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Refunds are a payout" icon="rotate-left">
    Not self-serve in the dashboard. An agreed refund is sent to the customer's wallet. Set `refund_address` when you initiate so you have somewhere to send it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The amount arrives wrong more often than you expect" icon="scale-unbalanced">
    Customers routinely send slightly less because their wallet deducts a withdrawal fee.

    Default behaviour refunds underpayments and credits overpayments. Both are configurable, and a line at checkout telling customers to check their provider's fee prevents more of them than any setting.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Settlement is not exact in stablecoin" icon="gas-pump">
    Roughly 1 USDT in gas. A 4 USD order does not settle as exactly 4 USDT — worth knowing if you reconcile per order.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who pays the fee" icon="receipt">
    Both the processing fee and the settlement fee can be passed to the customer, per link or as an account setting. Decide once — switching later changes every price your customers see.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
