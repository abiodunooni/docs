## Payment methods

Methods differ by currency and by channel. Call [List payment methods](/api-reference/ramp/rates-and-limits/list-payment-methods) for the live set rather than assuming.

| Currency | On-ramp | Off-ramp |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NGN | Virtual bank account, dynamic by default, static on request | Bank transfer |
| GHS | Mobile money | Mobile money and bank transfer |

In Ghana, mobile money reaches most consumer endpoints and settles in under five minutes. Bank rails reach formal account holders, settle more slowly and carry higher per-transaction caps. Consumer products usually lead with mobile money; higher-ticket and B2B flows often need bank rails.

| USD Coin | `usdc` | `bep20`, `erc20`, `solana` |

Additional assets, including XAUT and USAT, are available on some flows by arrangement. Confirm what is enabled on your account during onboarding.

<Tip>
  For predictable settlement timing, default to Tron or Polygon and use Ethereum only when the destination wallet requires it. Gas spikes on Ethereum mainnet delay sends by minutes, and Ramp queues rather than overpaying gas.
</Tip>

## Minimums

| Currency | Minimum |
| --- | --- |
| NGN | 3,000 |
| GHS | 50 |
| USDT | 5 |
| USDC | 5 |

Call [Get on-ramp limits](/api-reference/ramp/rates-and-limits/get-on-ramp-limits) and [Get off-ramp limits](/api-reference/ramp/rates-and-limits/get-off-ramp-limits) to validate against the live figures for your account, which include the maximum and the interval the limit is measured over.

```bash
curl 'https://ramp-be.quidax.io/api/v1/merchants/purchase_limits/buy?currency_symbol=ngn' \
  -H 'accept: application/json' \
  -H "x-private-key: $RAMP_SECRET_KEY"
```

```json
{
  "status": "ok",
  "message": "Fiat currency limit retrieved",
  "data": {
    "code": "ngn",
    "min_value": "2000.0",
    "max_value": "2000000.0",
    "interval": "daily"
  }
}
```

## Transaction tiers

Limits are set per user and determined by how much verification has been completed. Caps are enforced per corridor, so reaching a daily cap in one market does not affect another.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Widget">
    Tiers apply to your end users, and Quidax handles the upgrade path inside the flow.

    **NGN**

    | Tier | Daily limit | Requirement |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | Basic | ₦5,000,000 | BVN or NIN verification and a selfie |
    | Advanced | ₦75,000,000 | Proof of address |
    | Enhanced | $1,000,000 | Enhanced due diligence form, subject to manual review |

    **GHS mobile money**

    | Tier | Daily limit | Requirement |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | Basic | GHS 3,000 | Ghana Card verification and a selfie |

    | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

<Note>
  Limit increases are reviewed against your processed volume. Moving to a new tier involves a compliance refresh, including updated source-of-funds documentation. Ask your merchant success manager to start the review.
</Note>

### Handling a limit breach

A transaction that would exceed a limit is rejected before any money moves. Ramp never partially fills. Surface the message to your customer and, where you can, offer a smaller amount.

## Settlement times

| Direction | Rail | Typical time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| On-ramp | Bank transfer or virtual account | Under 5 minutes |
| Off-ramp | NGN bank payout | Under 2 minutes |
| Off-ramp | GHS mobile money | Under 5 minutes |

Times assume the local rail is available. Nigeria's instant rail runs continuously; some bank rails have processing hours, and transactions submitted outside them are queued.
