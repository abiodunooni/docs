Your customers pay in naira or cedis. You hold dollars. Local currency exposure stops at the point of collection instead of sitting on your balance sheet.

**Product:** [Ramp](/ramp) on-ramp — local currency in, stablecoin out.

## How it works

Your customer pays into a bank account Ramp generates for that transaction. Ramp converts and delivers stablecoin to a wallet you nominate.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the transaction">
    Create an on-ramp transaction with the fiat amount and a destination wallet address — or without one, if you are settling to a balance Quidax holds for you.

    **You get back** `status: pending`, a `TRX-...` reference and a `to_amount`. Store the reference separately from your own `merchant_reference`.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Refresh if the amount changes">
    If the customer edits the amount, refresh rather than creating a second transaction. Updated `from_amount` and `to_amount` return against the same `merchant_reference`, with no duplicate created.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm to get payment instructions">
    **You get back** a bank account and `amount_expected`, with the fee and VAT fields broken out.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Show amount_expected exactly">
    Display `amount_expected`, never the base `amount`, when the two differ. A customer who transfers the base amount has underpaid.
  </Step>

  <Step title="The customer pays from a name-matched account">
    Ramp confirms the deposit and the transaction moves to `processing`.

    **Do not credit the customer at this stage.** Handle repeated `processing` events safely.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Credit on settlement">
    **Expect** `buy_transaction.successful` with `data.status: completed`. Credit exactly once, and confirm the destination balance increased.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choose a settlement mode

| Mode | What happens | Suits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| To a wallet address you provide | Stablecoin is sent on-chain per transaction | You control custody |
| To a Quidax balance | Proceeds accrue in your merchant account | Fewer on-chain fees, sweep on your own schedule |

Settling to a balance and sweeping periodically costs less in network fees than settling each transaction on-chain.

## Add your own margin

Ramp lets you configure a percentage markup on the quoted rate, per market and per direction. It accrues on every transaction and you withdraw it from the dashboard.

## Widget or API

| | Widget | API |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who builds the interface | Quidax | You |
| Who verifies the end user | Quidax, once, in the flow | You, under your own licence |
| Time to launch | 3–10 business days | 1–4 weeks |

If you do not hold a licence covering customer verification, the widget is the faster and safer route — Quidax owns identity and sanctions screening on every user.
