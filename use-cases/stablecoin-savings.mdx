Customers in markets with volatile local currency want to hold dollars. A stablecoin savings product lets them fund in naira or cedis, hold in USDT or USDC, and withdraw back to local currency when they need it.

**Products:** [Ramp](/ramp) for the fiat legs, [Wallets](/wallets) for the balance each customer holds.

## The shape of it

```
Customer funds in NGN  →  Ramp on-ramp  →  USDT held in their sub-account
Customer withdraws     →  Ramp off-ramp →  NGN into their bank account
```

## Build it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a sub-account per customer">
    `POST /users` with your internal user ID in `reference`.

    Each customer's balance is then held and tracked separately, and you can show it directly rather than maintaining your own per-user accounting.

    <Card title="Sub-accounts" icon="users" href="/wallets/sub-accounts" horizontal />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fund in local currency">
    Run a Ramp on-ramp transaction and settle the stablecoin into that customer's sub-account.

    **Do not credit the customer's savings balance** until `buy_transaction.successful` arrives with `data.status: completed`. A transaction in `processing` has not settled.

    <Card title="On-ramp" icon="arrow-down" href="/ramp/on-ramp" horizontal />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Show the balance">
    `GET /users/{user_id}/wallets/usdt`

    Show `balance`. `converted_balance` gives the value in `reference_currency`, which is what most customers actually want to see — their dollars expressed in naira.

    **Never show `balance + locked`.** `locked` is committed elsewhere.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Let them add on-chain too">
    If customers already hold stablecoin, generate an address so they can fund directly.

    `POST /users/{user_id}/wallets/usdt/addresses?network=trc20`

    **Credit at `ACCEPTED` only**, after re-querying the deposit — not on `deposit.successful` alone.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Withdraw back to local currency">
    Run a Ramp off-ramp transaction from the customer's balance to their bank account.

    **Expect** `sell_transaction.successful`. NGN payouts typically settle in under two minutes.

    <Card title="Off-ramp" icon="arrow-up" href="/ramp/off-ramp" horizontal />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Decisions to make early

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where the customer's money legally sits" icon="scale-balanced">
    Holding customer funds is a regulated activity in most markets. This is the first thing to settle, before any code — confirm your licensing position during onboarding.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who verifies the customer" icon="id-card">
    The Ramp **widget** has Quidax verify each user once, inside the flow, including sanctions screening. The **API** has you verify under your own licence, with Quidax doing transaction-level monitoring.

    If you do not hold a licence covering verification, the widget is both faster and safer.

    <Card title="Widget or API" icon="split" href="/ramp#widget-or-api" horizontal />
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Limits will shape the experience" icon="gauge-high">
    Limits are per customer per rolling 24 hours, not per merchant and not per calendar day. There is no endpoint for remaining headroom.

    For a savings product where customers top up regularly, track consumption yourself so you can tell someone "you can add more at 15:00 tomorrow" rather than surfacing a bare rejection.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="USDT or USDC" icon="coins">
    Both are supported for on-ramp, off-ramp and custody. Pick one and be consistent — showing customers a balance split across two stablecoins invites questions you do not want to answer.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="This is not an interest product" icon="percent">
    Quidax provides the rails to hold and convert. Any yield you offer is your own arrangement, with its own regulatory consequences.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
