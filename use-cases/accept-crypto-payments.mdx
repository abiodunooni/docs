Your customer sees a price in naira or dollars. They pay in USDT, BTC or whatever else they hold. You receive one currency, converted on the way in.

No cards, no acquirer, no chargebacks.

**Product:** [Payments](/payments).

## Pick a channel

| | Checkout SDK | Payment links | API |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Effort | Embed a modal | None | Two calls plus webhooks |
| Who builds the payment screen | Basqet | Basqet | You |
| Single-use payments | Yes | **No — links are reusable** | Yes |
| Best for | Most storefronts | Invoicing, social selling | Custom checkouts |

Start with the checkout modal unless you have a reason not to. It handles the currency picker, address, QR code and countdown, and you get the same webhooks.

## Build it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Initialize at checkout">
    `POST /v1/transaction` with the amount, fiat currency and the customer's email. Uses your **public** key.

    **You get back** `status: INITIATED` and a reference. Nothing is owed yet, no rate locked.

    Put your order ID in `meta` — it comes back on every webhook, which saves you a lookup table.

    **Check first:** the amount must clear the minimum (₦2,000 or $1). Below it you currently get a `500`, not a validation error.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Initiate when the customer picks a currency">
    `POST /v1/transaction/{id}/pay` with `currency_id`. Uses your **secret** key.

    **You get back** `payment_amount`, `payment_address` and `qrCode`. Status moves to `PENDING`.

    **The rate locks here and the 60-minute expiry starts.** Call it when they are ready to pay, not on page load.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Display three things">
    The exact amount, the address, and the QR code.

    Add a line telling customers to check their wallet's withdrawal fee is not coming out of the total. This single sentence prevents more underpayments than any setting.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fulfil on the webhook">
    **Expect** `payment.received`. Fulfil then.

    Never fulfil on the customer returning to your site — someone who closes the tab after paying still paid, and someone who returns without paying has not.

    <Card title="Webhooks" icon="webhook" href="/payments/webhooks" horizontal />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Decide the awkward outcomes before launch">
    | Outcome | Default | Where to change it |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | Underpaid | Refunded, nothing credited to you | Settings → Business → Dispute Settings |
    | Overpaid | Credited to you in full | Same |
    | Abandoned | Expires after 60 minutes | Not configurable |

    Release reserved stock on `payment.abandoned`.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose what you hold">
    Convert everything to one currency as it arrives, or keep payments as paid and settle per currency.

    <Card title="Settlements" icon="building-columns" href="/payments/settlements" horizontal />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What is different from cards

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="There are no chargebacks" icon="shield-check">
    Crypto payments are final once confirmed on-chain. No bank or wallet provider can claw one back.

    This removes a whole category of fraud loss, but it also means disputes are resolved by you, not by an acquirer.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Refunds are a payout, not a reversal" icon="rotate-left">
    Refunds are not self-serve in the dashboard. An agreed refund is sent as a payout to the customer's wallet.

    Set `refund_address` when you initiate the transaction so you have somewhere to send it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The amount can arrive wrong" icon="scale-unbalanced">
    Customers routinely send slightly less than quoted because their wallet deducts a fee. Underpayments are normal, not exceptional — decide how you handle them before you launch.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Settlement is not exact in stablecoin" icon="gas-pump">
    Settling in USDT or USDC carries roughly 1 USDT in gas. A 4 USD payment does not land as 4 USDT.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
