Quidax pricing is based on the products you use and how your integration operates.

Your commercial proposal may consider your product, monthly transaction volume, transaction size, fiat currency, digital asset, market or corridor, payment method, settlement method, required liquidity, wallet and blockchain fees, integration model, and operational and compliance requirements.

<Note>
  Your agreed pricing and any discounts are configured on your account, and quotes are calculated from that configuration. Confirm it as part of setup, before you run your first live transaction. See [Quickstart](/get-started/quickstart).
</Note>

## Pricing components

Your total cost may contain one or more of the following.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Transaction fee" icon="receipt">
    A percentage or fixed fee charged when a transaction is successfully processed.

    This may apply to on-ramp transactions, off-ramp transactions, crypto payments, payouts, trades and conversions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Fiat processing fee" icon="banknote">
    A fee associated with collecting or paying out local currency through a bank, mobile-money or other payment rail.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Trading fee" icon="chart-candlestick">
    A fee charged when an order is executed through the Orderbook.

    The fee may depend on your commercial tier, whether you add or remove liquidity, the market, and the executed order amount.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Blockchain network fee" icon="link">
    A fee required to send a transaction over a blockchain network.

    Network fees can change based on the asset, the selected network, network congestion, the withdrawal amount and Quidax's applicable withdrawal-fee rules.

    <Warning>
      Retrieve current crypto withdrawal fees from the relevant fee endpoint rather than hard-coding them.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Spread or exchange rate" icon="arrow-right-arrow-left">
    Some conversion flows use an executable quote or exchange rate.

    The displayed rate may include the underlying market rate, the agreed commercial markup, available liquidity, the amount being converted, and the validity period of the quote.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Subscription or platform fee" icon="server">
    Some customised or enterprise integrations include a recurring platform, support or infrastructure fee.
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Volume-based pricing

Eligible businesses may receive tiered pricing based on monthly processed volume.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start at your agreed tier">
    Your transactions begin at the starting tier in your commercial terms.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Volume is reviewed">
    Processed volume is reviewed at the agreed interval.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pricing is adjusted">
    Pricing may move when your volume qualifies for another tier. Any grace period or pricing-retention rules are defined in your commercial terms.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Your signed commercial proposal remains the source of truth for your pricing. Fee fields returned in API responses, and the endpoints for retrieving current withdrawal fees and quotes, are documented on each product's pages.
</Note>
