Quidax supports businesses operating across African markets and selected global markets. Across our API products and OTC desk, coverage extends to **21 countries**, including markets in West, East, Central and Southern Africa.

Coverage differs by product, country, fiat currency, digital asset, blockchain network, payment method, settlement method, business model and regulatory approval. Your approved coverage is confirmed during onboarding.

## Fiat account requirements

Fiat collections and payouts are made to and from accounts belonging to the transacting party, which keeps every leg of a transaction traceable from source to destination. Design your beneficiary and account-collection flows around this from the start.

## Digital assets and networks

Supported assets and networks differ by product and may change over time. Quidax supports assets including BTC, ETH, USDT, USDC, XRP, LTC, BNB and SOL across applicable networks.

<Warning>
  Do not hard-code the full list of supported assets. Retrieve it from the relevant API endpoint so newly listed assets and networks appear in your product without a release.
</Warning>

Use the API to determine:

- Whether an asset is active.
- Supported deposit networks.
- Supported withdrawal networks.
- Minimum and maximum amounts.
- Required blockchain confirmations.
- Current withdrawal fees.
- Whether buying, selling, deposits or withdrawals are available.

The exact endpoints, response fields and per-asset limits are documented on each product's pages.
