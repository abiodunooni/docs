Quidax supports businesses operating across multiple African markets and selected global markets.

Coverage may differ by product, country, fiat currency, digital asset, blockchain network, payment method, settlement method, business model and regulatory approval. Your approved coverage is confirmed during onboarding.

## Geographic coverage

Quidax's digital asset infrastructure supports businesses across more than 20 countries, including markets in West, East, Central and Southern Africa. Current published coverage includes Nigeria, Ghana, Cameroon, Côte d'Ivoire, Kenya, Rwanda, Tanzania, Uganda and South Africa, among other markets.

## Fiat currencies

Fiat support is based on your approved integration and the available local collection, payout and settlement rails.

Currencies used across Quidax's institutional infrastructure include:

| Currency | Code |
| --- | --- |
| Nigerian naira | NGN |
| Ghanaian cedi | GHS |
| Kenyan shilling | KES |
| Central African CFA franc | XAF |
| West African CFA franc | XOF |
| Zambian kwacha | ZMW |
| Tanzanian shilling | TZS |
| United States dollar | USD |

<Note>
  Fiat collections and payouts are made to and from accounts belonging to the transacting party, which keeps every leg of a transaction traceable end to end. Design your beneficiary and account-collection flows around this from the start.
</Note>

## Digital assets and networks

Supported assets and networks differ by product and may change over time. Quidax supports assets including BTC, ETH, USDT, USDC, XRP, LTC, BNB and SOL across applicable networks.

<Warning>
  Do not hard-code the full list of supported assets. Retrieve it from the relevant API endpoint so newly listed assets and networks appear in your product without a release.
</Warning>

Use the API to determine:

- Whether an asset is active.
- Supported deposit networks.
- Supported withdrawal networks.
- Minimum and maximum amounts.
- Required blockchain confirmations.
- Current withdrawal fees.
- Whether buying, selling, deposits or withdrawals are available.
