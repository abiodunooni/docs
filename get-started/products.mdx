## Ramp

Ramp helps your customers move between local currencies and digital assets.

Use Ramp to:

- Let customers buy stablecoins or other supported assets with fiat.
- Let customers sell digital assets and receive fiat.
- Add deposits and withdrawals to an exchange or wallet.
- Collect local currency and settle your business in stablecoins.
- Fund stablecoin positions and pay recipients in local currency.
- Embed an on-ramp or off-ramp experience in your product.

### Widget or API

Ramp has two integration models. Your approved implementation determines which one you use.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Hosted widget" icon="window">
    Quidax renders the full experience and verifies each end user inside the widget, so you do not build or maintain a KYC flow. Fastest path to launch.
  </Card>

  <Card title="API" icon="code">
    You own the interface end to end. Because your customers transact through your own product, you verify them yourself and share evidence of your KYC process during onboarding.
  </Card>
</Columns>

Ramp supports **NGN** and **GHS**.

<Note>
  Fiat legs settle between Quidax and an account belonging to the transacting party. This keeps every transaction traceable from source to destination, and is worth designing for early if your product involves paying out to a beneficiary.
</Note>

## Payments

Payments is powered by Basqet, Quidax's crypto payment gateway.

Use Payments to:

- Accept supported digital assets from customers.
- Generate payment requests.
- Track payment statuses.
- Convert received payments.
- Settle in a supported fiat currency or digital asset.
- Send crypto payouts from the dashboard, or programmatically through the API.

Fiat settlement is supported across **NGN, GHS, ZAR, KES, XAF, XOF, UGX, ZMW, TZS, USD, EUR** and **GBP**.

<Tip>
  Payments is the only product with self-serve sign-up and a full sandbox, so you can build and test the complete flow while your business review is in progress.
</Tip>

## Wallets

Wallets provides the infrastructure for holding, receiving and sending supported digital assets. It is served by the Quidax Core API.

Use Wallets to:

- Create customer subaccounts.
- Retrieve balances.
- Generate deposit addresses.
- Receive digital assets.
- Send assets to internal or external wallets.
- Track deposits and withdrawals.
- Build custodial wallet experiences.
- Maintain balances for customers or business units.

<Note>
  If you plan to issue wallets to your own end users, onboarding includes an additional review of how you verify those users. Trading or holding assets on your own account does not. See [Onboarding](/get-started/onboarding).
</Note>

## Orderbook

Orderbook provides market data and APIs for executing trades across supported Quidax markets.

Use Orderbook to:

- Retrieve available markets.
- View prices and market depth.
- Place market and limit orders.
- Monitor open orders.
- Retrieve completed trades.
- Cancel eligible open orders.
- Convert between supported trading pairs.
- Access liquidity for bulk transactions.

Orderbook markets are quoted against **NGN, GHS, XAF, XOF** and **ZAR**.

## Wallets and Orderbook on one account

Wallets and Orderbook are served by the same Quidax business account. One login, one set of API keys and one webhook configuration covers both.

They are approved separately, though, because they carry different requirements:

| | Wallets | Orderbook |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What you are doing | Issuing and managing wallets, often for your end users | Trading and accessing liquidity on your own account |
| Onboarding | Standard business review, plus a review of how you verify end users if you issue wallets to them | Standard business review |
| Typical use | Custodial wallets, exchanges, brokerages, neobanks | Treasury, conversion, liquidity, bulk execution |

<Tip>
  Starting with Orderbook only and adding Wallets later is a common path. You keep the same account and credentials, and complete the additional review when you are ready to issue wallets.
</Tip>

## Which product should I use?

| Requirement | Recommended product |
| --- | --- |
| Let users deposit local currency and receive stablecoins | Ramp |
| Let users sell stablecoins and receive local currency | Ramp |
| Accept crypto as payment | Payments |
| Settle crypto payments in fiat | Payments |
| Create wallets for customers | Wallets |
| Send and receive digital assets | Wallets |
| Buy or sell through an exchange market | Orderbook |
| Build a crypto exchange or brokerage | Wallets + Orderbook + Ramp |
| Collect fiat and settle in stablecoins | Ramp |
| Collect crypto and settle merchants in fiat | Payments + Ramp |
| Execute large liquidity transactions | Orderbook or institutional liquidity |
