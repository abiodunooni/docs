## Who this is for

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Token issuers and projects" icon="rocket">
    You want your asset available to trade, hold and move across Quidax's supported markets.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Exchanges and partners" icon="building">
    You operate a venue or product with an asset you would like listed and supported on Quidax.
  </Card>
</Columns>

<Note>
  Already integrating and simply need an asset your customers are asking for? Raise it during onboarding or with your Quidax representative. Requests are assessed alongside your integration, so the asset can be ready when you launch.
</Note>

## How the review works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Submit your listing request">
    Email [support@quidax.com](mailto:support@quidax.com) with the information below.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Initial assessment">
    We review the asset against our listing, compliance and risk criteria.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Technical and liquidity review">
    We assess network support, custody requirements, market-making and liquidity arrangements.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Commercial and legal review">
    Where applicable, we agree terms, obligations and any regulatory considerations for the markets involved.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Listing and enablement">
    Approved assets are enabled for the relevant products and markets, and announced to our users and partners.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to include in your request

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Asset details" icon="coins">
    - Asset name, ticker and contract addresses.
    - Networks the asset is issued on.
    - Token standard and decimals.
    - Total and circulating supply.
    - Whether the asset is a stablecoin, and how it is backed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Project and team" icon="users">
    - Issuing entity and jurisdiction.
    - Team and ownership information.
    - Website, documentation and whitepaper.
    - Roadmap and current stage of the project.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Market and liquidity" icon="chart-line">
    - Existing exchange listings and liquidity venues.
    - Available depth on those venues.
    - Market makers you work with.
    - Any liquidity support you can commit to at launch.
    - Trading pairs you want on Quidax, and against which currencies.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Compliance" icon="shield-check">
    - Audits, attestations or reserve reports.
    - Smart contract audit reports.
    - Any regulatory approvals, opinions or restrictions.
    - Jurisdictions where the asset is restricted.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Demand" icon="megaphone">
    - Community size and activity.
    - Demand in the markets Quidax covers.
    - Expected volume once listed.
    - Any launch or marketing plans you would like to coordinate.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  The strongest submissions lead with demand and liquidity: who wants to trade the asset in our markets, and how depth will be maintained after listing.
</Tip>

## Once an asset is listed

Newly listed assets become available through the same endpoints as everything else. Businesses building on Quidax retrieve supported assets and networks dynamically, so a listing reaches integrations without them shipping a release.
