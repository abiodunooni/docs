Before processing live transactions, your organisation completes Quidax's business, compliance, commercial and technical onboarding.

Each product runs on its own platform, so onboarding happens per platform. If you use more than one product, you complete the relevant steps for each.

## Onboarding by product

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Payments">
    Payments onboarding is self-serve. Create your Basqet account, start building against the sandbox immediately, and submit your business details in parallel.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Create your Basqet account">
        Sign up with a business email address and verify it.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Build in the sandbox">
        Sandbox credentials are available straight away, so you can integrate while your business review is in progress.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Submit your business details">
        Upload your company documents and complete the compliance questionnaire from the dashboard.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Confirm settlement and pricing">
        Choose your settlement currency and confirm your commercial terms.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Get live keys">
        Once your business is approved, live credentials are enabled and you can switch your integration over.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Ramp">
    Ramp onboarding is guided by the Quidax team, so your implementation, corridors and limits are approved before launch.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Talk to the team">
        [Schedule a demo](/get-started/schedule-a-demo) and share your use case, markets and expected volume.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Choose your integration model">
        Confirm whether you are launching with the hosted widget or the API. With the widget, Quidax verifies each end user inside the flow. With the API, you verify your own customers and share evidence of that process during onboarding.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Complete business and compliance review">
        Submit your company documents, ownership details, compliance policies and flow-of-funds diagram.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Agree commercial terms">
        Confirm your pricing, corridors, currencies, settlement model and transaction limits.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Get platform access">
        Your Ramp dashboard is provisioned, and you create API keys and configure webhooks there.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Note>
      Integrating with the API? Prepare a short description of your customer verification process, the provider you use, and sample evidence of a verified customer record. Having this ready shortens the review.
    </Note>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Core API">
    The Core API covers Wallets and Orderbook. Onboarding is guided by the Quidax team.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Talk to the team">
        [Schedule a demo](/get-started/schedule-a-demo) and share the product you are building, the assets and markets you need, and your expected volume.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Complete business and compliance review">
        Submit your company documents, ownership details, compliance policies and flow-of-funds diagram.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Agree commercial terms">
        Confirm trading fees, discounts, limits and any liquidity commitments.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Get platform access">
        Your Core API account is provisioned. Designate the account owner who will hold dashboard access.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Enable two-factor authentication">
        Two-factor authentication with Google Authenticator secures the account. It is required before you can create API keys or process withdrawals.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Note>
      Core API dashboard access is held by a single account owner today. Choose an owner with a shared, organisation-controlled email address, and store the two-factor recovery details in your team's password manager so access is never tied to one person's device.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Information we may request

Requirements vary by country, entity type, product and risk profile.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Business and legal" icon="building">
    - Certificate of incorporation or registration.
    - Company constitutional documents.
    - Business licences.
    - Registered business address.
    - Ownership and organisational structure.
    - Tax identification information.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="People" icon="users">
    - Director and beneficial-owner information.
    - Identification documents.
    - Proof of address.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Compliance" icon="shield-check">
    - Regulatory or compliance policies.
    - Anti-money laundering controls.
    - Customer verification process and evidence, where you onboard your own customers.
    - Source-of-funds information.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Product and commercial" icon="chart-line">
    - Product flow and transaction diagrams.
    - Expected currencies, countries and transaction volumes.
    - Website, application or product materials.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  Gathering these documents before your first call is the single biggest thing you can do to shorten onboarding.
</Tip>
