If you already run a live product on another provider, you can move to Quidax incrementally. Most migrations run both providers in parallel for a short period, then shift volume once the new flow is proven.

## Plan your migration

<Steps>
  <Step title="Map your current flows">
    List every flow you run today: on-ramp, off-ramp, payments, wallet creation, deposits, withdrawals, trades and settlement. Note the volume and value of each.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Match each flow to a Quidax product">
    Identify which product covers each flow, and which platform it lives on.

    <Card title="Products" icon="grid-2" href="/get-started/products" horizontal />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete onboarding">
    Bring your existing volumes, corridors and compliance documentation to onboarding. An established track record usually speeds up commercial and compliance review.

    If you already verify your own end users, you will likely have most of what is asked for: your KYC provider agreement, a walkthrough of your in-app verification flow, and sample verified records.

    <Card title="Onboarding" icon="clipboard-check" href="/get-started/onboarding" horizontal />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Map your data model">
    Reconcile your concepts with Quidax's: customer to subaccount, wallet to wallet and address, payment to payment request, order to order. Decide how you will store Quidax references against your existing records.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Build behind a provider switch">
    Put the new integration behind a routing layer or feature flag so you can move flows one at a time, and roll back per flow.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Run a controlled pilot">
    Route a small share of live volume through Quidax, then reconcile fully before increasing it.

    <Card title="Testing" icon="flask" href="/testing" horizontal />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Shift volume and decommission">
    Increase the share of traffic in stages. Keep the previous provider available until every in-flight transaction has settled and reconciled.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to watch during migration

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="In-flight transactions" icon="clock">
    Transactions started on your previous provider must finish there. Route by the provider that created the transaction, not by the current default.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Balances and treasury" icon="scale">
    Fund your Quidax operational balances before you route volume, and rebalance across providers as traffic shifts.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Reconciliation" icon="list-checks">
    Run reconciliation across both providers for the full parallel period. Keep provider identity on every internal record so historical reporting stays correct.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Webhooks and statuses" icon="webhook">
    Status names and event shapes differ between providers. Normalise them into your own internal statuses rather than mapping provider values straight into your UI. Quidax's events, statuses and error codes are documented on each product's pages.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Customer experience" icon="users">
    Deposit addresses, supported networks, fees and limits may change. Communicate changes before they take effect, and stop advertising addresses you no longer monitor.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  Migrate your lowest-risk flow first, usually a read-only or low-value one. It proves credentials, webhooks and reconciliation end to end before any customer money depends on it.
</Tip>
