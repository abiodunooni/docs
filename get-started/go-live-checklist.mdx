Use this checklist before enabling your Quidax integration for customers. Work through it per platform if you are launching more than one product.

<Note>
  Items here apply across Quidax. Check them against the endpoints, statuses, error codes and limits documented on your product's own pages: [Ramp](/ramp), [Payments](/payments), [Wallets](/wallets) and [Orderbook](/orderbook).
</Note>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Business and compliance" icon="building">
    - Business verification is complete.
    - The approved use case matches the implemented transaction flow.
    - Required agreements have been signed.
    - Production countries and currencies have been approved.
    - Transaction limits have been confirmed.
    - Compliance escalation contacts have been assigned.
    - Customer terms and disclosures have been reviewed.
    - Any required customer verification process has been implemented. This applies where you integrate Ramp via the API, or issue wallets to your own end users.
    - The API agreement and any product-specific terms have been executed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Credentials and security" icon="key">
    - Production credentials are stored in a secrets manager.
    - Credentials are labelled by platform, so Payments, Ramp and business-platform keys cannot be confused.
    - Sandbox credentials are not used in production.
    - Secret keys are not exposed in frontend or mobile code.
    - Two-factor authentication is enabled for dashboard users, and recovery details are stored where more than one person can reach them.
    - Account ownership on Wallets and Orderbook is agreed, and the person holding the authenticator is documented and reachable.
    - API access is limited to authorised systems.
    - IP restrictions have been configured where supported.
    - Credential-rotation procedures have been documented.
    - Logs do not contain credentials or sensitive personal information.
    - All requests use HTTPS. Quidax requires secure HTTPS connections for API calls.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Transaction handling" icon="arrows-rotate">
    - Supported currencies and networks are retrieved dynamically where possible.
    - Amount and currency inputs are validated.
    - Transaction limits are checked.
    - Pending transactions are not treated as completed.
    - Failed and expired transactions are handled correctly.
    - Duplicate requests cannot result in duplicate customer credits.
    - Duplicate webhook events are handled safely.
    - Refund and reversal processes have been tested.
    - Customer-facing statuses use clear language.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Fiat flows" icon="banknote">
    - Collection and payout accounts belong to the transacting party, so funds are traceable from source to destination.
    - Your interface collects the account details you need up front, and validates them before submission.
    - Customers are told which account their payout will arrive in before they confirm.
    - Settlement timing is communicated in your customer-facing copy.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Wallets and blockchain transactions" icon="wallet">
    - The correct network is shown before a deposit or withdrawal.
    - Unsupported network deposits are clearly warned against.
    - Destination tags or memos are collected where required.
    - Withdrawal addresses are validated.
    - Blockchain confirmation requirements have been considered.
    - Crypto withdrawal fees are shown or accounted for.
    - Internal and external transfers can be distinguished.
    - Your withdrawal runbook accounts for the authenticator code required on Wallets and Orderbook, and names who holds it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Webhooks" icon="webhook">
    - A production webhook URL is registered on every platform you use, and each uses HTTPS.
    - Webhook authenticity is verified.
    - Events are acknowledged quickly.
    - Processing is idempotent.
    - Failed processing is retried.
    - Events are logged with sensitive fields removed.
    - A fallback polling or reconciliation process exists.
    - Alerts have been configured for repeated webhook failures.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Balances and settlement" icon="scale">
    - Required operational balances are funded.
    - Prefunding or reserve requirements have been met.
    - Low-balance alerts have been configured.
    - Settlement currencies and schedules are confirmed.
    - Fees are included in balance and reconciliation calculations.
    - Treasury owners have access to the required reports.
    - Funding and settlement escalation procedures are documented.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Reconciliation" icon="list-checks">
    - Every transaction has an internal unique reference.
    - Quidax references are stored against internal transactions.
    - Transaction states are reconciled regularly.
    - Wallet balances are reconciled.
    - Fees are recorded separately.
    - Settlement reports have been tested.
    - Missing or mismatched transactions trigger an alert.
    - Finance and Operations understand the reconciliation process.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Operational readiness" icon="headset">
    - Support teams understand the customer journey.
    - Internal teams can retrieve a transaction using its reference.
    - Support escalation contacts are documented.
    - Common customer issues have response procedures.
    - Monitoring is enabled for API errors and latency.
    - Incident owners and communication channels are defined.
    - A rollback or feature-disable process is available.
    - Initial production volume will be monitored closely.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Final approval

Before launching, confirm that:

- Quidax has approved the production integration.
- Production credentials have been tested.
- A controlled live transaction has succeeded.
- The transaction appears correctly in both systems.
- Settlement and reconciliation have been verified.
- Product, Engineering, Compliance and Operations have approved the launch.

<Tip>
  Launch to a small internal or invite-only group first, and hold there for a full settlement and reconciliation cycle before opening up to all customers.
</Tip>
