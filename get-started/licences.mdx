Quidax operates within applicable legal and regulatory frameworks, and reviews each business before enabling production services.

## Nigeria

Quidax is licensed by Nigeria's Securities and Exchange Commission as a Digital Assets Exchange. Quidax announced receipt of its provisional operating licence in August 2024 and currently operates as an SEC-licensed exchange.

## What this means for your integration

Quidax's regulatory status does not replace your organisation's own legal or regulatory obligations.

Depending on your business model and markets, you may still need to:

- Hold the appropriate local licences.
- Verify your customers.
- Screen customers and transactions.
- Monitor suspicious activity.
- Maintain transaction records.
- Provide required customer disclosures.
- Implement sanctions and restricted-country controls.
- Report applicable activity to regulators.
- Obtain legal advice before launching a regulated product.

<Note>
  On Ramp, the hosted widget verifies each end user inside the flow, so Quidax handles that step for you. If you integrate via the API, you verify your own customers and share evidence of your process during onboarding.
</Note>

## Restricted activity

You must not use Quidax products for activities prohibited by your Quidax agreement, applicable laws and regulations, sanctions requirements, or the policies communicated during onboarding.

Quidax may request additional information, restrict a transaction or suspend access where required for compliance, security or risk-management purposes.

## Regulatory enquiries

For licence documentation, compliance questionnaires or regulatory due diligence, contact your Quidax representative or the Compliance team through an approved channel.
