Quidax operates within applicable legal and regulatory frameworks, and reviews each business before enabling production services.

## Nigeria

Quidax is licensed by Nigeria's Securities and Exchange Commission as a Digital Assets Exchange. Quidax announced receipt of its provisional operating licence in August 2024 and currently operates as an SEC-licensed exchange.

## Other markets

In regions where Quidax does not hold a direct licence, we operate through licensed and compliant partners. That is how coverage extends across our supported markets while every transaction stays within the applicable local framework.

The arrangement is transparent to your integration — you build against the same APIs regardless of which market a transaction touches. The countries and currencies you are approved for are confirmed during onboarding.

## What this means for your integration

Quidax's regulatory status does not replace your organisation's own legal or regulatory obligations.

Depending on your business model and markets, you may still need to:

- Hold the appropriate local licences.
- Verify your customers.
- Screen customers and transactions.
- Monitor suspicious activity.
- Maintain transaction records.
- Provide required customer disclosures.
- Implement sanctions and restricted-country controls.
- Report applicable activity to regulators.
- Obtain legal advice before launching a regulated product.

<Note>
  On Ramp, the hosted widget verifies each end user inside the flow, so Quidax handles that step for you. If you integrate via the API, or issue wallets to your own end users, you verify those customers yourself and share evidence of your process during onboarding.
</Note>

## Verifying your own end users

Where you onboard end users and issue them wallets, we review how that verification works before enabling the flow. Depending on your setup, we ask for:

- Your master service agreement with your KYC provider.
- A video recording of your in-app KYC flow.
- Sample evidence of a verified customer record.

An API agreement is executed as part of enabling this. Trading or holding assets on your own account does not involve this review.

## Restricted activity

You must not use Quidax products for activities prohibited by your Quidax agreement, applicable laws and regulations, sanctions requirements, or the policies communicated during onboarding.

We may ask for more information, hold a transaction or suspend access where we need to for compliance, security or risk reasons.

We review every business before enabling production services. We do not work with businesses involved in criminal activity, or in sectors we are not able to support. Your Quidax representative confirms whether your business model is in scope during onboarding, so raise anything you are unsure about early.

<Warning>
  We also do not work with individuals or organisations on global sanctions lists, including those published by OFAC, the EU, HM Treasury and the UN, or with anyone identified as supporting terrorism.
</Warning>

Some countries are off-limits too. See [Prohibited countries](/get-started/coverage#prohibited-countries).
