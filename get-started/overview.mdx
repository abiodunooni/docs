Quidax provides the infrastructure businesses need to move between fiat currencies and digital assets, accept crypto payments, create and manage wallets, and execute trades through an order book.

Use our APIs to add digital asset functionality to your product without building the underlying exchange, liquidity, custody and settlement infrastructure from scratch.

## What you can build

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="On-ramp and off-ramp" icon="arrow-right-arrow-left" horizontal>
    Let customers buy digital assets with local currency, or sell and receive local currency.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Crypto checkout" icon="credit-card" horizontal>
    Accept crypto payments and settle in your preferred currency.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Wallet infrastructure" icon="wallet" horizontal>
    Create and manage wallets for your business or your customers, and move assets in and out.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Trading and conversion" icon="chart-line" horizontal>
    Convert between fiat, stablecoins and other digital assets, and place orders across supported markets.
  </Card>
</Columns>

You can also access liquidity for individual and bulk transactions, and combine any of the above into a single product experience.

## How the products work together

Quidax products can be used independently or combined. For example:

| What you want to build | Products |
| --- | --- |
| Let customers fund digital asset wallets with local currency | Ramp + Wallets |
| Accept crypto and settle merchants in fiat | Payments + Ramp |
| Create customer balances and trade on their behalf | Wallets + Orderbook |
| An exchange, brokerage or investment product | Ramp + Wallets + Orderbook |
| Accept, store and disburse digital assets | Payments + Wallets |

## How access is organised

Products are grouped across three platforms. Each platform has its own sign-up, dashboard, API keys and webhook configuration.

<Columns cols={3}>
  <Card title="Ramp" icon="arrow-right-arrow-left">
    **Ramp**

    Its own dashboard, credentials and webhooks. Activation is guided by the Quidax team.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Basqet" icon="credit-card">
    **Payments**

    Self-serve sign-up, its own dashboard, credentials, webhooks and a full sandbox.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Quidax business" icon="server">
    **Wallets** and **Orderbook**

    One business account serves both, with one set of API keys and one webhook configuration.
  </Card>
</Columns>

<Note>
  Wallets and Orderbook share a platform, but they are approved separately. Issuing wallets to your own end users carries additional requirements that trading on your own account does not. See [Onboarding](/get-started/onboarding).
</Note>

You only set up the platforms you use. If you integrate more than one product, plan for separate credentials and webhook endpoints per platform, and name your environment variables by platform so they cannot be confused.
