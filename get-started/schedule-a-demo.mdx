A demo call is the fastest way to confirm the right product mix for what you are building, and to get your account activated for production.

<Card title="Request a demo" icon="calendar" href="https://quidax.io/crypto-api/form/" horizontal>
  Tell us about your product and we will get back to you.
</Card>

You can register on any platform before the call, and we will pick up from wherever you have got to:

| Product | Sign up |
| --- | --- |
| Payments | [dashboard.basqet.com/signup](https://dashboard.basqet.com/signup) |
| Ramp | [ramp-merchant.quidax.io/sign-up](https://ramp-merchant.quidax.io/sign-up) |

<Note>
  Building with Payments? Onboarding is self-serve, so you can create your account and start in the sandbox today. Book a call if you want to discuss commercial terms or a larger rollout.
</Note>

## Before the call

Prepare the following where possible:

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Your business" icon="building">
    - A short description of your business.
    - The product you are building.
    - The countries where you operate.
    - The countries where your customers are located.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Your requirements" icon="sliders-horizontal">
    - Required fiat currencies and digital assets.
    - Expected monthly volume.
    - Expected transaction sizes.
    - Preferred settlement model.
    - Target launch date.
    - Any existing provider you intend to replace.
    - Whether you will issue wallets to your own end users.
  </Card>
</Columns>

<Tip>
  Bringing a simple flow-of-funds diagram to the first call, showing where money enters, where it converts and where it lands, usually removes a whole round of back-and-forth.
</Tip>
