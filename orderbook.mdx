Order Book is where you buy and sell on Quidax directly. You place orders against the live book, see the depth behind every price, and settle into the wallets you already hold.

<Note>
  Order Book is a **dashboard feature**. Sign in, pick a market, place an order — there is nothing to integrate.

  If you want to trade programmatically, or trade on behalf of your users, that is the [Wallets API](/wallets/trading).
</Note>

## Order types

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Market order">
    Fills immediately at the best price available on the book.

    **Use it when** getting the trade done matters more than the exact price — a small size, a liquid pair, or a position you need to close now.

    **The trade-off** is that you take whatever the book offers. On a thin pair, a large market order walks up the book and fills progressively worse as it consumes each price level.

    <Warning>
      A market order carries no price guarantee. Check the depth before sending a large one — the last traded price tells you nothing about what your order will actually average.
    </Warning>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Limit order">
    Rests on the book at the price you set, and fills only when the market reaches it.

    **Use it when** the price matters more than the timing — you are willing to wait, or you want to set a target and leave it.

    **The trade-off** is that it may never fill. A limit order sits until the market comes to it, you cancel it, or it is filled partially and the remainder keeps resting.

    A limit buy below the current price, or a limit sell above it, adds liquidity to the book rather than taking it — which is what makes you a maker.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Makers and takers

Every trade has two sides, and which one you are on determines what you pay.

| | You are a **maker** | You are a **taker** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What you did | Placed an order that rested on the book | Placed an order that filled against an existing one |
| Effect on the book | Added liquidity | Removed liquidity |
| Typical order | Limit, priced away from the market | Market, or a limit that crosses the spread |
| Fee | Lower | Higher |

A limit order is not automatically a maker order. If you place a limit buy **above** the best ask, it crosses the spread and fills immediately against resting orders — that makes you a taker, at taker fees.

<Tip>
  If you are trading regularly and price is not urgent, resting limit orders inside the spread earns you the maker fee on every fill. Over a month of volume that difference is usually larger than the spread you gave up waiting.
</Tip>

Your exact maker and taker fees depend on your commercial tier and monthly volume.

## Funding and cashing out in naira

<Warning>
  **Naira deposits and withdrawals are dashboard-only.** There is no API for moving NGN in or out of your Quidax account — fund your trading balance and cash out from the dashboard.
</Warning>

For programmatic local currency movement, use [Ramp](/ramp): on-ramp converts naira to stablecoin, off-ramp pays stablecoin out to a bank account or mobile money.

| You want | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Naira into your trading balance | Dashboard deposit |
| Naira out of your trading balance | Dashboard withdrawal |
| Naira in or out, programmatically | [Ramp](/ramp) |
| Crypto in or out, programmatically | [Wallets API](/wallets/withdrawals) |

## Minimums and precision

Two rules cause most rejected orders.

**Minimum order value** — the equivalent of **$1**, in whatever currency the pair is quoted in.

**Precision** — each pair caps the decimal places allowed on volume and price independently. For `BTC/USDT`, BTC volume takes 5 decimals, USDT volume 2, and price 2.

## Order states

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `WAIT` | Resting on the book, waiting to fill. |
| `DONE` | Fully executed. |
| `CANCEL` | Cancelled before completing. |

An order can fill across several trades — a large limit order may be matched by many smaller counterparties. `DONE` means the whole order completed, not that it was a single trade.

## Order book or swap?

| | Order Book | [Swap](/wallets/swap) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| You control the price | Yes, with limit orders | No, you accept a quote |
| Partial fills | Possible | Never |
| Speed | Immediate, or waits | Immediate |
| Best for | Size, price sensitivity, active trading | A simple one-off conversion |

If you just want to turn one currency into another and get on with your day, use Swap.
