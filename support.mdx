Email [support@quidax.com](mailto:support@quidax.com), or contact your assigned integration representative.

Support is organised per platform, so say which product you are using — Ramp, Payments, Wallets or Orderbook — in the subject line. That gets your request to the right team first time.

## What to include

The more of this you send up front, the faster a request is resolved:

- The product and platform involved.
- Your account or merchant identifier.
- The transaction reference, both yours and the Quidax reference.
- The timestamp, including timezone.
- The request and response, with secrets removed.
- The webhook payload, if one was received.
- What you expected to happen, and what happened instead.

<Warning>
  Never include API keys, secret keys or full customer identification documents in a support message. If you believe a credential has been exposed, rotate it immediately and then tell us.
</Warning>

## Production incidents

For a production incident:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Stop automatic retries">
    Pause retries if they could create duplicate transactions.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Preserve your evidence">
    Keep the relevant request, response and webhook logs.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm the current state">
    Retrieve the latest transaction status through the API before acting on it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Escalate">
    Email [support@quidax.com](mailto:support@quidax.com) or your designated escalation channel, and include all relevant transaction references. Mark the subject line as urgent so it is triaged as an incident.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Do not manually credit or debit a customer until the transaction state has been confirmed.
</Warning>
