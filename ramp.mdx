Ramp moves your customers between local currency and stablecoins. Integrate once, and offer on-ramping (local currency to USDT or USDC) and off-ramping (stablecoin to a local bank account or mobile money) without building your own banking, liquidity or treasury stack.

## Choose a channel

Ramp is delivered three ways. They share one merchant account and one set of credentials, so you can run more than one at a time.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Widget">
    A hosted pop-up you embed in your product. Quidax verifies each user inside the flow, then handles conversion and settlement.

    **Best for** consumer apps, wallets and platforms that want the fastest path to market.

    **Effort** low. Embed a script tag and call `window.ramp.initialize()`.

    Quidax owns end-user verification, banking relationships and liquidity. The widget carries Quidax branding, and users are verified as Quidax users even if they already completed verification with you.

    <Card title="Widget integration" icon="window" href="/ramp/widget" horizontal />
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="API">
    A headless REST API. You own the entire interface, and Ramp runs conversion and settlement in the background.

    **Best for** exchanges, fintechs, remittance platforms and aggregators.

    **Effort** medium. REST calls plus a webhook handler.

    You verify your own customers under your own licence, and Quidax performs transaction-level monitoring. Evidence of your verification process is reviewed during onboarding.

    <Card title="On-ramp guide" icon="arrow-down" href="/ramp/on-ramp" horizontal />
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Telegram">
    A chat-native channel running on the same compliance and settlement infrastructure, aimed at users in Telegram and the TON ecosystem.

    **Best for** distribution to chat-first audiences.

    **Effort** none. It is user-facing only, with no integration work.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip>
  A common pattern is to run both: the API for institutional or high-tier users where you want full control of the experience, and the widget for retail users where Quidax-hosted verification lifts conversion.
</Tip>

### Widget or API?

| | Widget | API |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who builds the interface | Quidax | You |
| Who verifies the end user | Quidax, inside the flow | You, under your own licence |
| Who holds the customer relationship | Shared, users are Quidax users | You |
| Integration effort | A script tag | REST plus webhooks |
| Typical time to launch | 3 to 10 business days | 1 to 4 weeks |

You can start on the widget and move to the API later. Your business verification and master services agreement carry over, and only the commercial schedule is re-papered.

## How a transaction works

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="On-ramp" icon="arrow-down">
    Your customer pays local currency and receives stablecoin.

    1. Create the transaction, with a destination wallet address or without one.
    2. Confirm it to generate a single-use bank account.
    3. The customer pays the exact amount shown.
    4. Ramp converts and delivers the stablecoin.
    5. Webhooks track it to completion.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Off-ramp" icon="arrow-up">
    Your customer sends stablecoin and receives local currency.

    1. Create the transaction with the customer's details.
    2. Attach the payout bank or mobile money account.
    3. Confirm it to generate a deposit address.
    4. The customer sends the exact amount.
    5. Ramp converts and pays out, and webhooks track it.
  </Card>
</Columns>
